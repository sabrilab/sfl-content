# Les 6 photos de la SFL : prompts

Pour Sabri, à générer toi-même (Magnific) si tu veux des images en attendant les vraies photos de Poleed. Même consigne de photographe pour les six : à coller en fin de chaque prompt.

**Consigne commune** : `shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400, warm soft colours, light grain, clear blue sky, midday sun, natural light, documentary sports photography, no text, no logo except the SFL crest on the jersey, realistic faces, no blur on faces`

| # | Plan | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Le héros | `low-angle shot of an amateur five-a-side football player standing with the ball under his foot, wearing a bottle-green jersey, red-brick apartment building of northern France entering the frame on the right, clear blue sky` |
| 02 | Le terrain | `wide shot at eye level of an outdoor five-a-side pitch surrounded by a fence, brick buildings behind, small players mid-game, white chalk lines on green artificial grass, clear blue sky` |
| 03 | Le détail | `ground-level close-up of worn black football boots next to a ball on artificial grass, white chalk line, low raking sunlight, shallow depth of field` |
| 04 | Le maillot | `a bottle-green football jersey with an embroidered crest, hanging on a hook against a red-brick wall, name and number on the back, soft sunlight` |
| 05 | Le portrait | `chest-up portrait of an amateur footballer looking at the camera, sweat, bottle-green jersey, clear blue sky behind, sharp face` |
| 06 | Le lieu | `empty grandstand of a racecourse next to a five-a-side pitch, fence, blue sky above, no people` |

À ne pas faire : fond studio, nuit, néons, flou artistique sur les visages, joueurs reconnaissables qui n'ont pas signé la charte.
